# School Life Dark Image Viewer v6

## Applied changes

- Changed image viewer into a full dark phone-screen viewer
- Added dark top bar with author context
- Added dark bottom dock/home-indicator area
- Image uses full available dark screen and keeps aspect ratio

## Behaviour

- When an admin taps an image, the viewer becomes a full dark phone screen.
- The top bar becomes dark and shows simple post context.
- The bottom dock area becomes dark with a small home-indicator style bar.
- The image fills the available viewing area while keeping its real aspect ratio.
- Tap the image background or close button to exit.

## Scope

- School admin image viewer only.
- No Parent/Teacher message changes.
- No API route changes.
- No database/schema changes.
